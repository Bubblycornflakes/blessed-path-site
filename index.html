Gracious Path

Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated 1 November 2026

Gracious Path is a daily gratitude and kindness app. Everything you write in it stays on your own device. We do not collect, store, see or sell any of it.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your gratitude notes, actions, reflections, garden and preferences are saved only on your device.
  • The app makes no network requests. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it yourself.

What the app stores, and where

Gracious Path keeps one small file on your device containing your daily gratitude notes, the kindnesses you chose or wrote in and whether you completed them, your weekly reflection answers, and your preferences (categories, filters and reminder time). On iPhone this file lives in the app's own storage, which is protected by your device passcode and is included in your iCloud or iTunes backup if you have backups switched on. We have no access to your backups; they are between you and Apple.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information. We do not know who you are.
  • We do not send your data to any server. There is no server.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting services, advertising networks or social media tools.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not share or sell anything, because we hold nothing to share or sell.

Things you can choose to do

  • Reminders. If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. This uses your phone's own notification system and never involves a server. You can turn it off at any time in Preferences or in your phone's Settings.
  • Home Screen widget. If you add the Gracious Path widget, the app shares a small summary with it on your phone: today's suggested kindness (until you complete it), your garden's stage and how many days you showed up this month. Anyone who can see your Home Screen can see this, so the widget never shows your gratitude notes, reflections or any kindness you wrote in yourself, and the Lock Screen version shows progress only. The summary stays on your device like everything else.
  • Kindness cards. Cards are drawn on your device. They contain a quote, a milestone, an invitation or your month's progress, and only include one of your gratitude notes if you explicitly turn that on and pick it. Sharing a card opens your phone's share sheet; where it goes from there is your choice.
  • Export. "Export a copy of my data" creates one readable file of everything in the app and hands it to your phone's share sheet so you can keep it wherever you like.
  • Erase. "Erase everything on this device" permanently deletes the app's data from your device. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Tips. The iPhone app offers optional tips to the developer through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy and tells us only that a purchase happened, never who made it. Tips unlock nothing and are entirely optional. They are not charitable donations.
  • Giving. The Giving screen lists humanitarian organisations with links to their own websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies; nothing about your use of Gracious Path is passed to them.

Using the app sensibly

Gracious Path offers gentle suggestions for small acts of kindness. They are ideas, not instructions: use your own judgement about what is safe and appropriate for you and the people around you, and never put yourself at risk to complete one. The app is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice and is not a substitute for professional support. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support service.

The app is provided free, as it is, without warranties of any kind. Because your data lives only on your device, keeping a copy is your responsibility; the Export option is there for that. Links to charities are provided for convenience only; we do not endorse, verify or take responsibility for them, and any donation is between you and the organisation.

Children

Gracious Path does not collect information from anyone, including children. It contains no accounts, chat, purchases or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes any of the above, for example an optional way to sync between your devices, this page will be updated first and the app will ask for your consent before anything is sent anywhere.
