How it works

A minute a day, and something good happens.

01

Notice

Write three small things you're grateful for. A warm cup of tea counts.

02

Do

Get one practical kindness, from 133 ideas: hold the lift, thank a colleague, call someone you miss. Swap it, or write in your own.

03

Grow

Every kindness grows your garden, from a seed in the sand to a tree in bloom. It never shrinks.

Garden of Goodness

A garden that only grows.

Each kindness you complete adds a little life to a hand-drawn desert garden. Miss a day and nothing is lost: there are no streaks here, only progress.

Widgets

A gentle nudge on your Home Screen.

Widgets in every size show today's kindness, your week and your garden, plus a daily thought on the Lock Screen. Your notes never appear on them.

Days, remembered

Look back, kindly.

  • A calm calendar of every day you showed up
  • A weekly look back: what you did, and the words you wrote most
  • Kindness cards to share a thought or a milestone
  • An optional daily reminder that steps aside once you're done

Our promise

Your notes stay on your phone.

Gracious Path has no server. Everything you write is stored only on your device and backed up by your own iCloud, if you use it. We can't see it, so we can't sell it.
